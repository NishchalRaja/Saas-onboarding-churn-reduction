# Stakeholder Analysis
## Onboarding Strategy — FlowStack

---

## 1. Stakeholder Map

| Stakeholder | Role | Power | Interest | Engagement Approach |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| COO (Executive Sponsor) | Approves budget, owns company-wide efficient-growth goal | High | High | Monthly steering update; ROI framed in ARR terms |
| Head of Customer Success | Owns CSM playbook and team capacity | High | High | Weekly working sessions during design and pilot |
| VP Product | Owns in-app onboarding UX and roadmap priority | High | Medium | Bi-weekly check-ins; co-owns requirements sign-off |
| Engineering Lead | Owns build feasibility and sprint capacity | Medium | Medium | Involved from Planning phase onward for scoping |
| Sales / Account Management | Cares about renewal conversations and expansion revenue | Medium | Medium | Informed at rollout; feedback loop post-launch |
| Finance | Tracks ARR, churn, and NRR reporting | Medium | Low | Informed via business case sign-off and quarterly results |
| Frontline CSMs | Execute the revised, health-score-triggered outreach process | Low | High | Involved in playbook design; trained before pilot |

## 2. RACI Matrix

| Activity | COO | Head of CS | VP Product | Eng Lead | Sales |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Business case approval | A | C | C | I | I |
| Strategy sign-off | A | R | R | C | I |
| Requirements (BRD) sign-off | I | A | R | C | I |
| Onboarding flow build | I | I | A | R | I |
| Health-score model build | I | C | A | R | I |
| CSM playbook update | I | R | I | I | C |
| Pilot go/no-go decision | A | R | R | C | I |
| Full rollout | I | R | R | R | I |

*R = Responsible, A = Accountable, C = Consulted, I = Informed*

## 3. Power/Interest Grid Summary

- **Manage Closely (high power, high interest):** COO, Head of Customer Success — these two are the primary decision-makers and the ones whose teams' day-to-day work changes most.
- **Keep Satisfied (high power, lower interest):** VP Product, Finance — need visibility and sign-off but aren't running day-to-day execution.
- **Keep Informed (lower power, high interest):** Frontline CSMs, Sales — directly affected by the new process and a key source of ground-truth feedback, but not decision owners.
- **Monitor (lower power, lower interest):** Engineering Lead beyond the build phase — critical during delivery, less involved once shipped.

## 4. Engagement Cadence

- **Steering (COO, Head of CS, VP Product):** Monthly, or at each phase-gate in the [Project Lifecycle](./Project_Lifecycle.md)
- **Working team (Eng, Product, CS ops):** Weekly during Design and Build
- **Frontline CSMs:** One playbook-training session before pilot start, then a standing feedback channel during the pilot window
